A selected, favorable test within one dataset

The panel contains 24 signatures: 12 queries and 12 references, four labels across three cell contexts at one time/dose. Each cell/label/role has one signature and one source-prefix group. No fitting, centering, imputation or tuning was added; the score is cosine over 978 directly measured landmarks. The inherited within-/between-prefix medians each contain one value here, so they do not provide robust multi-group averaging.

Query and reference signature IDs, replicate IDs, plates, brews and prefixes are disjoint. Those proxies derive from a shared naming system and are not five independent guarantees. The three cell contexts span only two project-pair types, within the same LINCS2020 beta release. Shared processing and controls may remain; the number of independent biological units cannot be established from these metadata. This is neither blind nor independent-facility validation.

Positive controls were selected because they are useful transcriptional perturbations. The favorable panel does not represent everyday compounds in general. A shared well row also remains for one label. The limited position rule and gene permutations do not remove all confounding. RNA, protein changes and cell viability are separate measurements.

What was verified

Plan and code were frozen, reviewed by independent Codex and actual Claude, then committed and pushed before the one numerical acquisition. A separate NumPy implementation checked 1,008 cosine scores and 25,684 stored scalar values; maximum numeric discrepancy was 2.22 × 10⁻¹⁶. An independent acquisition audit checked all 26 saved HTTP responses and the same 24 selected vectors, without new requests.

The next research question

This panel records one working example under selected conditions with unresolved confounding. Before interpreting everyday compounds, a separate fixed test must examine why the earlier two-label panel failed and whether retrieval transfers to other measurements. A biologically justified target state and exposure/selectivity checks remain separate requirements for useful candidate research.

Fixed methods, with their limits retained.

A descriptive comparison of RNA changes between paired doses in the same cells within one public dataset, using five constituents.

Comparison and metrics

RNA values are L1000 Level 5 replicate-aggregated standardized scores, not raw RNA abundance, fold change or protein abundance. The registered 210-query evaluation uses all 978 directly measured landmark genes; a custom direction uses 20–978 of them. No inferred genes, centering, imputation, training or tuning are used.

Success requires all five candidates to be scoreable and the correct constituent to be the unique best. All 210 queries remain in the denominator.

Ties use midranks. The reciprocal rank is zero when the correct candidate is unscoreable or the five-candidate panel is incomplete.

Controls and source grouping

One fixed-seed gene-position permutation is applied to all queries. The theoretical random reference is not an independent measured control.

The earlier two-label comparison selected quercetin for all six queries. We check whether median aggregation contributed to that bias, using saved similarity scores only. MRR averages the inverse rank of the true condition, using the midrank for ties. Strict top1 requires a unique winner. Top5 requires the worst rank of the truth's tie group to be at most five. Fractional top1 is supplementary: it gives 1/tie-size credit only when the truth belongs to the highest-scoring tie.

Why dose-only unique top1 is zero: each absolute dose is shared by four, five or nine candidates, forcing ties. Its structural fractional-top1 baseline is 4 / 27 (14.8%). A strict-top1 rate of zero alone does not establish a strong negative control.

Read unique top1 alongside ties and the full ranking.

Fractional top1 is 15.4% for RNA and 14.8% for dose alone. The joint dose-and-position control gives top5 189 / 324 and MRR 0.306; RNA gives 115 / 324 and 0.282. Higher MRR means a better truth rank. Unique top1 alone does not establish that RNA outperforms every source of information.

What this technical check can support

This is a technical diagnostic within a new source study. It does not establish cross-site performance, external replication of the earlier two-compound model or causal mechanisms. The three recorded replicates are not confirmed as three independent treatment days or batches.

Exposure is six hours: four compounds at 50, 500 and 1000µM, and five at 1, 50 and 500µM. Food intake, achievable human exposure, human benefit, treatment and anticancer efficacy were not measured. The iPSC-derived cardiac model is not a matched normal-tissue control for the tumor-derived lines.

The eight DMSO well positions are shared by every recorded block. The position and dose controls test limited shortcuts; they cannot guarantee removal of all technical effects.

Metadata discrepancies remain: the paper describes four replicates while GEO lists three; the series describes five controls while samples describe eight. This uses the three deposited replicate blocks and eight controls. Wells come only from sample titles; opaque description column IDs are never interpreted as positions. Six controls have title well D2 and a column-ID suffix D01, which is retained in the record. The separate THP-1 and HAEC candidates remain on hold while dose, background, controls or other conditions are unresolved.

Every source feature row remains a separate coordinate in its original order, including duplicate labels. No gene annotation or connection to the earlier 978-feature model is made. Each candidate requires references from both other replicates, and all 27 candidates must be finite to score a query. Unscoreable queries retain zero credit in the fixed denominator of 324.

When a condition is entirely correct or entirely incorrect, condition-level aggregates or confusion counts in the public JSON can reveal per-cell/replicate success or failure. These deductions remain possible even though individual IDs and scores are omitted.
