METHODS / OPEN QUESTIONS

Fixed methods, with their limits retained.

A descriptive comparison of RNA changes between paired doses in the same cells within one public dataset, using five constituents.

Comparison and metrics

RNA values are L1000 Level 5 replicate-aggregated standardized scores, not raw RNA abundance, fold change or protein abundance. The registered 210-query evaluation uses all 978 directly measured landmark genes; a custom direction uses 20–978 of them. No inferred genes, centering, imputation, training or tuning are used.

Success requires all five candidates to be scoreable and the correct constituent to be the unique best. All 210 queries remain in the denominator.

Ties use midranks. The reciprocal rank is zero when the correct candidate is unscoreable or the five-candidate panel is incomplete.

Controls and source grouping

One fixed-seed gene-position permutation is applied to all queries. The theoretical random reference is not an independent measured control.

This gene-position permutation is not a control matched for plate / brew. The difference between 185 observed and 47 permuted unique label recoveries cannot distinguish a constituent-specific response from a pattern shared by a measurement group.

The metadata diagnostic was added after the primary method was fixed and before numerical parsing or scoring. The ID concern was already known, so it is not a blinded validation. The 210 rows and primary metrics are unchanged. Exact plate-set equality identifies 194/210; any plate overlap identifies 210/210.

Eight correspondence states

Five structure-corresponding constituents are included. Citric acid and L-ascorbic acid remain unmatched; sulforaphane remains unspecified in stereochemistry. Physical sample identity is separately unverified.

The earlier 821 figure counts structure-corresponding signature metadata rows across two releases. The current 339 rows contain imported values from one GSE70138 release; these are different denominators. Cross-release overlaps are not independent experiments. The older 78-condition protein retrieval uses a separate measurement and catalog.

The next validation

A later design must place the same constituents across different source groups, with correspondence and exclusion rules fixed before reading values. Validity of a desired cellular state, human exposure and safety are separate questions.

Source ID prefixes alone identify all 210/210 correct labels, so RNA label recovery in this panel cannot separate compound-specific response from source grouping. This does not prove a batch effect exists in RNA values. Shared studies, measurements and controls may explain similarity; reciprocal queries are dependent. No quality-based selection was applied. Healthy-state restoration, normal-cell selectivity, achievable human exposure, intake safety, and food or treatment efficacy are unvalidated. RNA and protein are separate measurements. Source examples do not establish equivalence between a food and a pure constituent.

Healthy target undefined

Fictional desired directions do not mean restoration to a normal cell. RNA and protein are separate measurements.

Human exposure unverified

Whether experimental concentrations are achievable in people, spare normal cells or are safe to consume has not been established.

Food and treatment effects unvalidated

Foods contain multiple substances in different amounts and forms, with changes in the body. Cultured-cell records of a pure constituent cannot establish food or treatment efficacy.
