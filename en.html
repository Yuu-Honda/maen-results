Protein retrieval: evaluation and limitations

MCF7 breast-cancer cells, 12-hour treatments, 78 conditions and two replicates. Because metformin appears at five concentrations, this means 78 conditions for 74 named substances in the source table, rather than 78 different chemicals. Measurements concern protein abundance, not activity or phosphorylation.

Positive measurements in Table S2 were log2-transformed. Cosine similarity used pairs with at least 100 jointly observed accessions. Missing values were neither imputed nor equated with zero. Interpreting the values as linear relative abundance is an assumption informed by the paper’s methods; the workbook itself does not declare units. This is not a reconstruction of the paper’s gene aggregation, minimum-value imputation or ComBat-corrected matrix.

57.8% of measurement entries are missing. Missingness patterns are shared within TMT groups, as are DMSO controls. Jaccard similarity of observed accession sets (a value-free control of missingness positions) and value permutations that preserve masks serve as controls. The uniform-rank reference is a mathematical comparison, not independent probabilistic trials or a p-value. Separating replicates does not remove measurement dependence.

External Table S3 uses gene-level Log2FC signatures. Of 20 signatures, 19 had candidates and one had none. Eleven mapped using source names were evaluated; nine with uncertain identity or absent from the catalog were excluded from identity accuracy. Name matching does not prove exact chemical identity, and cell type, time and concentration differ.

RNA prediction: evaluation and limitations

Official CellMiner 2.15, HG-19, date label 09-11-2025, log2(FPKM+1). 23,808 gene records × 60 cells, no missing values. Zero is not treated as missing. Provider-side FPKM processing is used as supplied; raw data is not reprocessed.

Related cell lineages are reduced to representatives; 44 development cells are compared across the same 20 splits. Outer training has 28–30 cells, calibration 6–8 and evaluation 7–8, shared by all six drugs. Median imputation, top-500 variance selection, scaling and PCA are fitted only within each training partition. PCA 2/5 and Ridge alpha 1/10/100 are selected by inner 3-fold MAE. Added controls use tissue shrinkage 0/1/10/100 with inner CV and one fixed RNA-row permutation. The permutation also breaks tissue correspondence. Four tissue candidates and six molecular candidates do not equalize model-class complexity. Evaluation results are not used for tuning.

In the original three-model comparison, RNA had lower MAE than the drug mean in 19/20 splits and fixed tissue in 18/20. The added tuned tissue model was worse than fixed tissue in 18/20. These splits overlap and are not independent trials.

Molecular six-drug simultaneous interval coverage averaged 87.2%, but half-widths were broad: 1.548–2.533 pGI50. Coverage is descriptive; exchangeability is unestablished. “55/55” refers to input-name matching, not prediction accuracy. GI50 censoring and measurement conditions remain unresolved. Independent final evaluation is incomplete, and the eight excluded held-out cells were also used previously.

Drugs: doxorubicin, cisplatin, paclitaxel, fluorouracil, methotrexate and etoposide.

Protocol freezing, environment and terms of use

Protein-search code, configuration and input hashes were frozen before scoring at commit 012e2c5. The 41 passing tests are a local execution record. Input parsing took 6.498 s and computation 1.558 s, excluding download and plotting.

RNA added controls froze code, inputs, configuration and environment before scoring at commit ce5a4fb. There were 21 passing local tests and a 17.237 s run on one CPU thread. In the original RNA evaluation, the protocol was frozen before scoring, but imported code and environment hashes were additionally pinned after results. We do not claim every dependency was frozen in advance. That original run recorded 14 local tests and 11.224 s. These records are not CI or a validation of the whole system.

Original data and full transformed matrices are not published. The protein paper is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; a separate data license has not been confirmed. No blanket redistribution permission is assumed for this or NCI data. Each provider’s terms apply.
