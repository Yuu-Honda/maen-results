METHODS AND LIMITS

What was read, and what remains unused?

1. References and correspondence

Eight fixed chemical references were compared with the full 4,532-key PRISM structure inventory. Molecular-graph and full Standard InChIKey correspondence does not identify the physical sample or show food benefit. Salts, stereochemistry, and tautomers were not silently collapsed.

2. RNA source-audit record and follow-up

The initial audit inspected perturbagen and signature metadata from Broad LINCS L1000 GSE92742 and GSE70138 (2017-03-06), checking files against published checksums. The 821, 304, 168 and other counts here are signature metadata rows across two releases. Expression values, connectivity scores and quality metrics were not obtained or analyzed during that audit. The follow-up measured RNA lookup uses 339 signatures from GSE70138 only, as a separate process; it does not replace these metadata counts.

3. Releases and measurement types

Counts remain separate by release. Different release records do not establish independent facilities, experiments, or biological replicates. RNA expression is not a substitute for protein abundance. The existing protein ranking was not changed.

4. Additional protein sources

We located treatment-and-measurement studies related to the eight examples, but confirmed zero exact chemical forms and imported zero records into the old catalog. The scale and normalization stage of the curcumin S2 table remain unresolved. These gates must be settled before numerical analysis.

5. Prior-exposure disclosure

In a separate protein-source audit, two numerical rows were accidentally displayed while inspecting the header of a quercetin supplement PDF. Reading stopped, and the values were not used for signatures, ranking, or statistical tests. Future reuse must disclose that exposure and cannot be called wholly unused or blinded.

6. What comes next

The follow-up RNA diagnostic fixed comparison conditions, missing-value handling, cosine similarity and the fixed-seed permutation control before reading response values. Measurement-group labels alone identify the constituents, so checks across other measurement groups or facilities remain necessary. Healthy-state restoration, achievable human exposure, normal-cell effects, and food or mixture benefits remain unassessed.
